# Exhibition Histories

## Modern Impressionism Showcase – 2018
The 2018 Modern Impressionism Showcase featured several cornerstone works from European collections. Among the highlighted pieces were:
- *Water Lilies* exhibited in Gallery 5.
- *Self Portrait* displayed in Hall A.
- *Landscape Study* showcased in the East Viewing Corridor.
This exhibition emphasized the role of natural light and evolving brushwork throughout the late 19th century.

## Abstract Dialogues – 2020
The Abstract Dialogues series brought together a selection of digital and mixed‑media works. Notable pieces included:
- *Abstract Form* presented in the Central Digital Hall.
- *Morning Field* displayed in Level 2 West.
- *Guernica* featured in the South Atrium (artist attribution not provided in the exhibition record).
The series explored recurring geometric patterns and the influence of spatial tension.

## Sculpture and Form – 2016
An exploration of sculptural techniques and material experimentation. Works included:
- *Persistence of Memory* shown in the Time & Memory Gallery.
- *Sunset Over Hills* presented in the Upper Sculpture Hall.
