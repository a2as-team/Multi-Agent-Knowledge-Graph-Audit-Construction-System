# Provenance & Loan Notes

## Provenance Record – Starry Night (AW001)
The artwork *Starry Night* has a well‑documented ownership trail. 
It was first held in a private Dutch collection before being transferred to a European gallery in the late 19th century. 
Its presence in the Orangerie Museum is recorded in several catalogues. 
Previous owner: **Johannes Vermeer Estate**

## Provenance Record – Starry Night (Alternate Entry)
The artwork *Starry Night* is listed in another archival document with a contrasting ownership history. 
It states that the work was previously kept within the **Hendrick de Vries Family Collection**, conflicting with earlier provenance documents.
